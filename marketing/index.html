About MotiPaper

We Know How Indian Presses Work

Born out of frustration with WhatsApp forwards and paper registers, MotiPaper was built by people who've spent time on the press floor.

❌ Jobs get lost or forgotten

No single place to see all active jobs. Operators rely on memory or WhatsApp — until something slips.

❌ Quotations are guesswork

Margins are unclear, rates vary job-to-job, and estimates are never compared against actual costs.

❌ Challans say "NILL"

Manual numbering breaks down. Challans go out without numbers. Disputes follow.

❌ Payments slip through

No running ledger means you don't know who owes what — or for how long — until month-end.

Our Story

From the Press Floor, For the Press Floor
